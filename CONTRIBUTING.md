# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. The project is intended to remain a practical, free field reference for recreational pilots.

## Good contributions

- Correct outdated FAA or CBO information
- Repair or update QR destinations
- Improve clarity without changing meaning
- Improve print readability or accessibility
- Add a genuinely useful field-reference card
- Report duplicated, contradictory, or ambiguous information

## Regulatory or safety changes

Please include:

- the card and text being changed;
- the proposed replacement wording;
- a link to a primary source (FAA, statute/regulation, or FAA-recognized CBO guidance);
- the date the source was checked.

Do not rely on an AI answer, forum post, social-media post, or YouTube video as the sole source for a regulatory change.

## Design constraints

Unless a future release intentionally changes the format, cards should remain:

- 3 × 5 inches, landscape;
- 1500 × 900 px at 300 DPI for raster masters;
- readable after printing and lamination;
- consistent with the existing ring-hole safe zone and visual hierarchy;
- free of unofficial FAA, FTCA, or Flite Test logos that could imply endorsement.

## QR validation

Any QR added or changed should be generated programmatically and decoded from the final exported card before release.

## New cards

A new card should answer a question a pilot is likely to ask **in the field**. If the content belongs in a long tutorial rather than a glanceable reference, it may be better as documentation instead of a card.
