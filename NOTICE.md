# Project Notice

FPV Pilot Field Cards is an independent community project.

It is **not an official publication of the Federal Aviation Administration, Flite Test, or the Flite Test Community Association**, and no endorsement by those organizations is implied.

The cards are designed as quick-reference summaries. Pilots remain responsible for checking current regulations, airspace restrictions, authorization requirements, and the safety guidelines of the FAA-recognized CBO under which they operate.
