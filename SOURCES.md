## Source policy

For regulatory or safety-critical changes:

1. Prefer current FAA, statute, regulation, or recognized-CBO source material.
2. Record the source URL and review date.
3. Avoid changing safety-critical wording solely from forum posts, videos, or social media.
4. Re-run QR validation after changing any QR destination or card artwork.
